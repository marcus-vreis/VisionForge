# ADR template

Copy the block below to the end of `docs/dev/DECISIONS.md`, take the next free
number and fill it in. The rules (when an ADR is required, the status values)
are at the top of `DECISIONS.md`.

```markdown
## ADR-NNN — Short title in the imperative or as a statement

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Status:** Proposed
**Relates to:** ADR-XXX (optional)

**Context:** the problem and the constraints, in a few sentences.

**Decision:** what is done, concretely enough to be checked against the code.

**Consequences:** what changes for users, tests and other modules; what gets harder.

**Alternatives considered:**

- *Option A.* Why not.
- *Option B.* Why not.
```

Status is one of `Proposed`, `Accepted`, `Superseded by ADR-NNN`,
`Superseded in part by ADR-NNN`, `Amended by ADR-NNN` or `Rejected`.
